Research Experience

Fall 2024–Present Graduate Research Assistant
University of Central Florida — Ah-Tah-Thi-Ki Museum Archival Project
  • Conducting curatorial and interpretive research on archival newspaper clippings to surface and address colonial framings in historical media coverage of the Seminole Tribe of Florida.
  • Designing and maintaining a structured textual mapping of unstructured archival sources — categorizing themes, language patterns, and contextual metadata.
  • Collaborating within a multidisciplinary research team of graduate students, faculty, and museum staff.
  • Partnering directly with tribal stakeholders to align research methodology with community-defined principles of data sovereignty and ethical engagement.
  • Synthesizing findings into written interpretive outputs for both academic and public museum audiences.

Digital Projects

Comparative LLM Interpretation Study
Autoethnographic comparative case study of how Translinguo, ChatGPT, and Claude interpret a photograph when supplied with an explicit authorial framework (Context, Abstraction, Concept). Findings rendered as an interactive HTML/JavaScript concept map with citation infrastructure.
Combinations — Textile-to-Poetry Generative System
A p5.js generative artifact that unravels textile visuals into derived poems and poetic structures. Critical-making project examining how each coding medium constrains identity expression.
Interactive Game on Writing as Process
A four-room Bitsy-style pixel game (HTML/CSS/JavaScript) externalizing the writing process across Blank Page, Brainstorm, Draft, and Toolbox environments; brings game-based pedagogy into critical-making research.
Women, Textiles, and Rhetorical Patterns
Data visualization project using deliberate empty cells to render legible the rhetorical absence of women in textile archives — operationalizing absence as data.
AgenticDemoing
Public GitHub Pages repository (acsuarez84.github.io/AgenticDemoing) hosting digital humanities artifacts developed via AI-assisted coding workflows.
